# Baggage

- Economy: 15 kg checked baggage and 7 kg cabin baggage per passenger.
- Business: 25 kg checked baggage and 10 kg cabin baggage per passenger.
- Excess checked baggage is charged at 600 rupees per kg at the airport.
- Lost or delayed baggage: report it at the airport baggage desk, and the baggage team will also call back within 24 hours of a report made here.

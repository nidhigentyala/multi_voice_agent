# Changes and cancellations

- A booking can be moved to another flight on the same route until 4 hours before departure.
- If the new flight costs more, the passenger pays the difference. If it costs less, the difference is not refunded.
- Cancelling 24 hours or more before departure gives a full refund.
- Cancelling less than 24 hours before departure gives a 50 percent refund.
- A flight that has already departed cannot be cancelled or refunded.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days.
- If SkyBridge cancels a flight, the passenger can choose a free change or a full refund.
